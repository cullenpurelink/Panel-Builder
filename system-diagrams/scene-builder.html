Scene Builder — prototype

Composites a room master with product icons (already generated by product-icon-generator.html) and draws cable-coloured connection lines between them, the same way the hand-drawn classroom masters do it — just data-driven instead of hand-placed. This is a single hard-coded scene for the first checkpoint, not a general scene editor yet. Room furniture anchors below were found by cross-checking the room SVG's own transform matrices (the display) and visual estimation (table, credenza) — approximate by design, not measured to the millimetre.
